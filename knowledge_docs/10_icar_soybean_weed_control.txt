Soybean is highly sensitive to weed competition during the critical crop-weed competition window spanning the first 30 to 45 days after sowing (DAS). Uncontrolled weeds (such as Echinochloa colona, Digera arvensis, Cyperus rotundus) can cause yield losses exceeding 40-60%.

Integrated Herbicide and Cultural Protocols:
1. Pre-Emergence Herbicide Application:
- Apply Pendimethalin 38.7% CS @ 1.75 - 2.0 liters/ha or Diclosulam 84% WDG @ 26-30 g/ha dissolved in 500 liters water/ha within 48 hours of sowing on moist soil surface.
2. Early Post-Emergence Herbicide Options (15 to 20 DAS):
- For mixed weed flora (broadleaf and grasses): Apply Imazethapyr 10% SL @ 1.0 liter/ha or Propaquizafop 2.5% + Imazethapyr 3.75% ME @ 2.0 liters/ha.
3. Cultural Suppression:
- Inter-row cultivation with a sweep blade or power weeder at 25-30 DAS breaks soil crust and eliminates escapes while promoting root aeration. Maintain optimal plant canopy density (400,000 to 450,000 plants/ha) to ensure early row closure.
