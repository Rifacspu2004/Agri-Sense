Grain sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) is one of the most drought-resilient C4 cereal crops, exhibiting low transpiration ratios and deep fibrous root architectures capable of penetrating up to 1.8 meters.

Dryland Yield Maximization:
1. Seed Priming and Sowing Depth:
- Hydro-priming seeds for 10 hours in water followed by surface shade drying increases germination vigor under limited moisture. Sow at 4-5 cm depth into moist soil seedbeds.
2. In-situ Moisture Conservation:
- Implement compartment bunding or tied ridging during pre-monsoon rains to capture 80-90% of episodic rainfall. Mulching with sorghum straw or dry grass @ 4-5 tonnes/ha conserves moisture through the grain-filling period.
3. Pest Warning - Shoot Fly (Atherigona soccata):
- Early uniform sowing within 7 to 10 days of monsoon onset escapes the peak oviposition window of shoot fly. Treat seeds with Imidacloprid 70% WS @ 5 g/kg seed.
