FAO statistical analysis of international agricultural productivity categorizes yield potentials across major staple crops based on climatic zones and input regimes:

1. Maize (Zea mays):
- Rainfed semi-arid potential: 2.5 - 4.5 tonnes/ha (25,000 - 45,000 hg/ha).
- High-input irrigated temperate/tropical: 8.0 - 12.0+ tonnes/ha.
- Key limiting factor: Water deficit during the silking/tasseling window leads to high kernel abortion.

2. Wheat (Triticum aestivum):
- Cool temperate / irrigated zones: 5.0 - 8.5 tonnes/ha.
- Sub-tropical irrigated (Indo-Gangetic plain): 3.5 - 5.5 tonnes/ha.
- Limiting factor: Terminal heat stress during grain filling (temperatures exceeding 32°C accelerate senescence).

3. Potatoes (Solanum tuberosum):
- High-performing global average: 25 - 45 tonnes/ha (250,000 - 450,000 hg/ha).
- Limiting factor: Night temperatures exceeding 20°C drastically curtail tuber bulking; susceptibility to Phytophthora infestans (Late Blight).

4. Cassava (Manihot esculenta):
- Average smallholder yield: 10 - 15 tonnes/ha.
- Improved clonal selections with fertilizer: 25 - 35 tonnes/ha.
- Resilience: Exceptional tolerance to drought and acidic soils; susceptible to Cassava Brown Streak and Mosaic viruses.
