The System of Rice Intensification (SRI) developed and adapted by Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) maximizes paddy grain yield while reducing irrigation water usage by 30 to 40 percent.

Core SRI Pillars:
1. Young Seedlings: Transplant 8 to 12-day-old young seedlings (with 2 leaves and the seed sac intact) with minimal root trauma instead of conventional 25-30 day seedlings.
2. Single Plant Establishment: Plant single seedlings per hill in a square pattern (25 cm x 25 cm spacing) to encourage profuse tillering (up to 30-50 productive tillers per plant) and unimpeded root proliferation.
3. Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD): Avoid continuous flooding. Keep fields moist during the vegetative phase by irrigating only after hair cracks appear on the soil surface, re-flooding to a shallow depth of 2.5 cm. Provide 5 cm standing water exclusively during the critical panicle development and grain fill stages.
4. Mechanical Weeding: Run the rotary or cono-weeder 3 to 4 times at 10-day intervals starting from 10 days after transplanting (DAT). This incorporates weeds as green manure, churns soil, and aerates the root zone.
SRI methods regularly demonstrate 20-30% higher yields (6.5 to 8.5 tonnes/ha) compared to traditional puddling and flood irrigation.
