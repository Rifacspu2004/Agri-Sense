Late Blight caused by the oomycete Phytophthora infestans remains the single most economically destructive pathogen in potato and tomato production, capable of destroying entire canopies within 7-10 days under cool, humid conditions (relative humidity > 90%, temperature 10-20°C).

Epidemiology and Control Protocols:
1. Certified Seed Tubers: Plant exclusively certified disease-free seed tubers. Discard any tubers showing dry sunken brown rot or internal marbling.
2. Preventive Prophylactic Spraying:
- Apply protective contact fungicides such as Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0-2.5 kg/ha or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 kg/ha prior to canopy closure or immediately upon weather forecast of persistent cloudiness and drizzle.
3. Curative / Systemic Intervention:
- If lesions (water-soaked dark spots with white mildew border on the underside of leaves) appear, switch to systemic translaminar fungicides: Cymoxanil + Mancozeb @ 3.0 g/liter or Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/liter.
- De-haulm (cut and remove potato vines) 10-14 days before harvest to eliminate sporangia transfer from foliage to tubers during lifting.
