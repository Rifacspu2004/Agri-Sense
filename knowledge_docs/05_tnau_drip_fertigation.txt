Fertigation delivers fully water-soluble fertilizers directly into the root zone via pressurized micro-irrigation systems, achieving 85-95% water and nutrient-use efficiency compared to 40-50% in broadcast flood irrigation.

Key Protocol Guidelines:
1. System Maintenance: Install disc filters (120 mesh / 130 micron) and sand media filters to eliminate physical suspended particles. Periodically perform acid treatment with hydrochloric acid (pH 4.0) to dissolve calcium and magnesium carbonate precipitates in dripper emitters.
2. Fertilizer Compatibility: Never mix calcium-containing fertilizers (such as Calcium Nitrate) with phosphates (MAP/DAP) or sulfates (Magnesium Sulfate/Potassium Sulfate) in the same stock injection tank to avoid insoluble precipitates.
3. Scheduling by Phenological Stages:
- Establishment & Vegetative Stage: High phosphorus (12:61:0) and moderate nitrogen to stimulate primary and secondary root branch systems.
- Flowering & Fruit/Grain Setting Stage: Balanced 19:19:19 formulation supplemented with micronutrients.
- Maturity & Ripening Stage: High potassium (0:0:50 or 13:0:45) to enhance starch synthesis, fruit size, brix, and drought resistance.
